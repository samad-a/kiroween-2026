🎃 Kiroween 2026 is open for hacking👻

Kiro’s annual Halloween-themed hackathon has returned, and building starts now. Last year, thousands of developers used Kiro to bring dead tech back to life, stitch together unexpected technologies, and create hauntingly good experiences. This year, it’s your turn.

Show us how you use Kiro to push boundaries. Work across a crew of agents in Kiro Crew, add structure with specs, customize workflows with hooks or steering, streamline with Kiro powers, or bring in new capabilities with MCP. Pick a category, build something wicked, and compete for $100,000 in prizes.

You have until Friday, December 4, 2026 at 2:00 PM Pacific Time to submit.

Only new projects are eligible. You must build your project during the Submission Period (October 30 to December 4, 2026). Projects you started before October 30, including Kiroween 2025 projects, are not eligible.

6. Judges & Criteria
Sponsor and Administrator reserve the sole right to determine the eligibility and judging methodologies for all submissions. This process may utilize expert panels, peer review, automated AI-driven analysis, or any combination thereof to ensure efficient, fair, and objective evaluation. Eligible submissions will be evaluated by a panel of judges selected by the Sponsor (the “Judges”). Judges may be employees of the Sponsor or third parties, may or may not be listed individually on the Hackathon Website, and may change before or during the Judging Period. Judging may take place in one or more rounds with one or more panels of Judges, at the discretion of the Sponsor. 

 

Stage One) The first stage will determine via pass/fail whether the ideas meet a baseline level of viability, in that the Project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably applies the required tools featured in the Hackathon.

 

Stage Two) All Submissions that pass Stage One will be evaluated in Stage Two based on the following equally weighted criteria (the “Judging Criteria”):

Potential Impact (20% - 10 points)
Does the project make a credible case for solving a real problem for a real audience? Does the solution address that problem based on what’s demonstrated? How useful, intuitive, and accessible is it? Would someone actually use this?
Technological Implementation (20% - 10 points)
How thoroughly and skillfully does the project use Kiro? Was there a thoughtful strategy to AI development? Was a variety of Kiro tools used impactfully? Does the project show a deep understanding of Kiro features? Was there experimentation?
Design (20% - 10 points)
Does the project deliver a complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept? Is the design intuitive, polished, and intentional?
Innovation/Idea (20% - 10 points)
How creative and novel is the concept, and does the project differ from existing concepts? Are any unusual technologies or public resources (like datasets) leveraged or combined in a unique way?
Presentation (20% - 10 points)
Does the video clearly demonstrate the project working end-to-end? Does the pitch communicate what problem is solved, who it’s for, and why it matters? Is the overall presentation easy to follow? Is the presentation creative and memorable?
The scores from the Judges will determine the potential winners of the applicable prizes. The Entrant(s) that are eligible for a Prize, and whose Submissions earn the highest overall scores based on the applicable Judging Criteria, will become potential winners of that Prize. 

Overall Prizes will be awarded to the highest-scoring eligible Submissions across all categories.
Category Prizes will be awarded to the highest-scoring eligible Submission in each category indicated in the Project Submission based on the same Judging Criteria.
Best Startup Project Bonus Prize will be awarded to the highest-scoring eligible Submission with an eligible Startup profile and required details shared in the Project Submission.
Best Ghost of Kiroween Past Bonus Prize will be awarded to the highest-scoring eligible Submission that indicates 2025 Kiroween participation and is verified by Devpost.
Best Kiro Crew App Bonus Prizes will be evaluated by the Kiro team from among the top 50 eligible Submissions that include a Kiro Crew app GitHub repo link. The top Kiro Crew app will be evaluated at the sole discretion of the Kiro team based on the value/usefulness and technical innovation/idea.
Best Student Project for each category will be awarded to the highest-scoring eligible Submission in the category indicated in the Project Submission and also includes eligible student details in the Submission. 
Best Demo Bonus Prize will be awarded to the highest-scoring eligible Submission in the Presentation criterion that is also in the top 10% of overall eligible Submission scores. Any ties will be reviewed according to the Tie Breaking rules for the Presentation criterion alone.
