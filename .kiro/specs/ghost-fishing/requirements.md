# Ghost Fishing: requirements

Phase 3. A single-player mini-game at the fountain, so there is no sync work.

1. The player SHALL start a round from the fountain.
2. A round SHALL last 60 seconds.
3. The player SHALL cast, then hit a timing bar to catch something.
4. Catches SHALL include pumpkins, bones and rare spooky fish, worth different coins.
5. WHEN a round ends, the server SHALL add the earned coins to the player's balance.
6. A coin balance SHALL NEVER go negative.
7. The shop SHALL sell about 6 hats/outfits and 6 room decorations for coins.
8. A player SHALL NOT be able to buy the same item twice.
9. Items and coins SHALL be saved server-side (SQLite).
10. A stranger SHALL be able to play without any explanation.
