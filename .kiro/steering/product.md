# Club Kiro: Product

## Pitch
Flash-era virtual worlds died with Flash. Club Kiro brings one back as a haunted town where you hang out as a ghost. An AI Caretaker keeps it safe by reading chat in context instead of using a keyword list.

- Resurrected: browser virtual worlds, the genre Flash made possible.
- Modern: real-time multiplayer with no plugin, plus an AI character who greets, hosts and moderates.
- Problem: keyword filters miss meanness with no bad words in it, and block harmless jokes.

Hero moment: a cruel message with no swear words gets caught, and a harmless joke a filter would block goes through.

## Audience
Teens and up. Moderation is fairly strict, but jokes and banter are fine.

## Tone
Cosy-spooky. Deep purple night, soft glow, flickering lanterns, parchment and tombstone panels. Never gory, never scary-scary.

## Core loop
Join, float around the plaza, chat, play Ghost Fishing, earn coins, buy hats and decorations, decorate your haunted room, visit others.

## World
- Shared haunted town square, up to 20 ghosts per plaza (open a second copy when full).
- Ghosts are original, drawn in SVG. No Disney or Club Penguin names, art or music.
- The Caretaker is an ancient, kind ghost who has looked after the town for centuries. Gentle, in-character nudges.
- Name: Club Kiro. Backup: Club Boo.

## UX principles
- Readable text, good contrast, keyboard focus for menus.
- Reduced-motion option and a mute button for sound.
- A stranger should be able to play without explanation.
